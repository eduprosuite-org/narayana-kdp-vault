\section*{Final AP Statistics Exam Readiness Checklist}
\begin{lessonbox}[The 2027 Score-5 Checklist]
Review this final checklist 24 hours before your examination:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{TI-84 Fresh Batteries / Fully Charged:} Put new batteries or charge overnight; ensure \texttt{DiagnosticOn} is enabled.
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{C-U-S-S Framework Memorized:} Center, Unusual Features, Shape, Spread always in context.
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{P-A-N-I-C for Confidence Intervals:} Parameter, Assumptions, Name interval, Interval calculation, Conclusion in context.
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{P-H-A-N-T-O-M for Significance Tests:} Parameter, Hypotheses, Assumptions, Name test, Test statistic, Obtain $p$-value, Make decision in context.
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{Variances Always Add:} $\sigma_{X \pm Y} = \sqrt{\sigma_X^2 + \sigma_Y^2}$. Never subtract standard deviations!
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{Large Counts for $\chi^2$:} All \textbf{expected counts} $\ge 5$ (never check observed counts!).
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{Degrees of Freedom Precision:} $df = n - 1$ for 1-sample $t$; $df = n - 2$ for slope $t$; $df = (r-1)(c-1)$ for two-way tables.
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{Never Say ``Prove'':} Fail to reject $H_0$; never claim $H_0$ is proven or accepted.
\end{itemize}
\end{lessonbox}
