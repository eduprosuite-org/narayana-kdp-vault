Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT)

Hypothesis: $f(x)$ is continuous on the closed interval $[a, b]$.

Conclusion: $f(x)$ takes on every value between $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ at least once in $(a, b)$.

FRQ Phrase: "Since $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and $f(a) < k < f(b)$, by IVT there exists $c \in (a, b)$ such that $f(c) = k$."

Mean Value Theorem (MVT)

Hypothesis: $f(x)$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ AND differentiable on $(a, b)$.

Conclusion: There exists at least one $c \in (a, b)$ such that $f'(c) = \frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$.

FRQ Phrase: "Because $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$, by MVT there exists $c \in (a, b)$ where $f'(c) = \frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$."

Extreme Value Theorem (EVT)

Hypothesis: $f(x)$ is continuous on the closed interval $[a, b]$.

Conclusion: $f(x)$ attains both an absolute maximum and an absolute minimum on $[a, b]$.

FRQ Phrase: "By EVT, since $f$ is continuous on the closed interval $[a, b]$, absolute extrema must occur at critical points or endpoints."

Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC)

Part 1 (Accumulation): $\frac{d}{dx}\left[\int_a^x f(t)\,dt\right] = f(x)$.

Part 2 (Net Change): $\int_a^b f'(t)\,dt = f(b) - f(a)$.

FRQ Phrase: "The amount at $t = b$ equals the initial amount at $t = a$ plus the net change: $f(b) = f(a) + \int_a^b f'(t)\,dt$."

AP Calculus BC Series Convergence Test Decision Tree

1. nth-Term Test for Divergence

If $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n \neq 0$, the series DIVERGES.

Warning: If limit equals 0, the test is INCONCLUSIVE.

2. Geometric Series Test

$\sum a r^n$ converges if and only if $|r| < 1$. Sum $= \frac{a}{1 - r}$.

3. p-Series Test

$\sum \frac{1}{n^p}$ converges if $p > 1$, and diverges if $p \le 1$.

4. Ratio Test (Power Series & Factorials)

$\lim_{n \to \infty} \left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right| = L$. Converges if $L < 1$, diverges if $L > 1$.
