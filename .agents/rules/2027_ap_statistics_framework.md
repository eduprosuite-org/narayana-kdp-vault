# 2027 AP® Statistics Official Exam Structure & Curriculum Framework

This document defines the permanent rules for all AP® Statistics books in the Master Review Series starting with the 2026-2027 academic year.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Official 2027 Digital Exam Structure

### Section I: Multiple Choice
- **42 Questions** | **1 Hour 30 Minutes (90 Mins)** | **50% of Exam Score**
- **Question Structure:**
  - Individual discrete multiple-choice questions
  - Item Sets: 2 to 3 questions tied to a common real-world scenario, dataset, or experiment.
- **Calculator:** Graphing calculator with statistical capabilities (TI-84 Plus CE) OR built-in Desmos graphing calculator through Bluebook.
- **Calculator Policy Update for 2027:** Nongraphing scientific calculators are STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED, even those with statistical computation capabilities.

### Section II: Free Response
- **4 Questions** | **1 Hour 30 Minutes (90 Mins)** | **50% of Exam Score**
- **Holistic Rubric Scoring:** Each question scored 0–4 points using Essentially Correct (E), Partially Correct (P), Incomplete (I).
- **Question Types:**
  - **Question 1: Multi-Focus on Practices 1 & 2**
    - Practice 1: Formulate Questions (1.A)
    - Practice 2: Collect Data (2.A, 2.B, 2.C, 2.D, 2.E)
  - **Question 2: Multi-Focus on Practices 3 & 4**
    - Practice 3: Analyze Data (3.A, 3.B, 3.C, 3.D, 3.E)
    - Practice 4: Interpret Results (4.A, 4.B, 4.C, 4.D)
  - **Question 3: Statistical Inference**
    - Significance Hypothesis Test OR Confidence Interval (PANIC / PHANTOM framework)
    - Verifying conditions, formulas, calculations, and concluding in context (Practice 4.E, 4.F, 4.G).
  - **Question 4: Multi-Focus Synthesis on Practices 2, 3, & 4**
    - Comprehensive statistical investigation synthesizing experimental/sampling design, numerical/graphical analysis, probability models, and inference under uncertainty.

## 🎯 3. The 4 Statistical Course Skills & Practices

### Practice 1: Formulate Questions (5%–10%)
- **1.A:** Determine a valid investigative question that requires a statistical investigation.

### Practice 2: Collect Data (20%–30%)
- **2.A:** Identify information to answer a question or solve a problem.
- **2.B:** Justify an appropriate method for ethically gathering and representing data.
- **2.C:** Identify appropriate statistical inference methods.
- **2.D:** Identify types of errors and relationships among components in statistical inference methods.
- **2.E:** Identify the null and alternative hypotheses.

### Practice 3: Analyze Data (25%–35%)
- **3.A:** Construct tabular and graphical representations of data and distributions.
- **3.B:** Calculate summary statistics, relative positions of points within a distribution, and predicted responses.
- **3.C:** Calculate and estimate expected counts, percentages, probabilities, and intervals.
- **3.D:** Calculate means, standard deviations, and parameters for probability distributions.
- **3.E:** Calculate appropriate statistical inference method results.

### Practice 4: Interpret Results (25%–35%)
- **4.A:** Describe and compare tabular and graphical representations of data, as well as summary statistics.
- **4.B:** Justify a claim based on statistical calculations and results.
- **4.C:** Describe distributions and compare relative positions of points within a distribution.
- **4.D:** Interpret statistical calculations and results to assess meaning or a claim.
- **4.E:** Justify the use of a chosen statistical inference method by verifying conditions.
- **4.F:** Interpret results of statistical inference methods.
- **4.G:** Justify a claim based on statistical inference method results.
