\chapter{Standard Interpretation Sentence Templates}

Memorize and fill in these exact templates on your Free-Response Questions to capture 100\% of rubric points:

\begin{templatebox}[Template 1: Confidence Interval for a Parameter ($\mu$ or $p$)]
\emph{"We are [C\%] confident that the interval from [Lower Bound] to [Upper Bound] captures the true [proportion / mean] of [context of the problem with units]."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 2: Confidence Level ($C\%$)]
\emph{"If we were to take many random samples of the same size from this population and construct a [C\%] confidence interval from each sample, about [C\%] of the resulting intervals would contain the true [population mean / proportion in context]."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 3: $p$-Value in Context]
\emph{"Assuming that the null hypothesis is true (that [state $H_0$ in context]), there is a [value of $p$-value] probability of getting a sample statistic as extreme as or more extreme than the one observed in our study purely by random chance."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 4: Significance Test Conclusion (Reject $H_0$)]
\emph{"Because the $p$-value ([value]) is less than the significance level $\alpha = [0.05]$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing statistical evidence that [state $H_a$ in context]."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 5: Significance Test Conclusion (Fail to Reject $H_0$)]
\emph{"Because the $p$-value ([value]) is greater than the significance level $\alpha = [0.05]$, we fail to reject $H_0$. There is NOT convincing statistical evidence that [state $H_a$ in context]."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 6: Standard Error of the Slope ($\text{SE}_b$)]
\emph{"In repeated random samplings of size $n$, the estimated slope of the sample regression line will typically vary by about [value of $\text{SE}_b$] [units of $y$ per unit of $x$] from the true slope of the population regression line relating [response] to [explanatory]."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 7: Coefficient of Determination ($r^2$)]
\emph{"About [value of $r^2 \times 100\%$] of the variation in [response variable in context] is accounted for by the linear model relating it to [explanatory variable in context]."}
\end{templatebox}

\begin{templatebox}[Template 8: Standard Deviation of Residuals ($s$)]
\emph{"When using the least-squares regression line with [explanatory variable] to predict [response variable], our predictions will typically be off by about [value of $s$] [units of $y$]."}
\end{templatebox}
