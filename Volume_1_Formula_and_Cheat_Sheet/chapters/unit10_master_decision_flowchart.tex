\section{How to Pick the Exact Inference Procedure Every Time}

Follow this 3-question decision process:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*:}]
    \item \textbf{What type of data / variable(s) are you analyzing?}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Categorical (Counts, percentages, proportions) $\to$ Go to Proportion / Chi-Square.
        \item Quantitative (Numerical measurements, averages, means) $\to$ Go to $t$-Test / Slope.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{How many samples / groups are being compared?}
    \item \textbf{Are you estimating an unknown value (Confidence Interval) or testing a claim (Significance Test)?}
\end{enumerate}
